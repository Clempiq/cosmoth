Songe Veille Noire

Songe

Players take turns as the storyteller, picking a card and giving a clue. The others slip in a card from their hand that could match, then everyone votes to find the storyteller’s card. If everyone finds it, or no one does, the storyteller scores nothing: the whole art is in the clue.

Players
3 to 6, each on their own phone
Cards
61 illustrations, each in its own style
Languages
English, French, Spanish
